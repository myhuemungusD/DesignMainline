Built like a part, not a trinket.

Printed at 0.12mm layer height so the text reads clean and the surface doesn't feel like a prototype.

Living with it

  • Wipe with a dry or slightly damp cloth. Skip solvents and alcohol wipes — they'll dull the finish.
  • Keep it out of a hot car. PLA+ softens around 60 °C / 140 °F, and a dashboard in July beats that.
  • Friction fit — it slides on and stays on. No adhesive, nothing to peel.
  • Scuffed it? Reorder the same design for less. Just reply to your order email.

Fitment: sleeves are modeled per device. Pick your model at checkout. Device brand names are used only to describe compatibility — Design Mainline is an independent maker and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any device manufacturer.

Questions

Before you order.

Will it fit my device?
Each sleeve is modeled for a specific device, and you pick your model at checkout. If yours isn't on the list, email jason@designmainline.com — if we can get our hands on one to measure, we'll model it.
How long can my text be?
14 characters, including spaces. The preview auto-scales the text down as you type so you can see exactly how it lands. Short and bold reads best.
Can I put a logo or artwork on it?
Yes — that's a custom job rather than a standard order. Email the artwork and we'll quote it. We can't print trademarked logos or copyrighted characters we don't have rights to, so if it's someone else's mark, we'll have to pass.
Is the text going to wear off?
No. The text is printed into the part as raised geometry — it's the same piece of plastic as the sleeve. Nothing to peel, scratch off, or fade.
What if I don't like it?
Because every sleeve is made to order with your text on it, we can't resell it — so custom orders are final sale. That said, if it arrives damaged, misprinted, or the text is wrong on our end, we reprint it free. Just send a photo.
Can I order a bunch?
Yeah. Shops, events, and team orders get a volume rate — email jason@designmainline.com with quantity and we'll come back within 24 hours.
