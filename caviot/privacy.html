Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · Last updated October 29, 2026

Caviot Studio is made by Design Mainline LLC ("we"). This policy covers the Caviot Studio apps for iPhone, iPad, Android and Mac, and the website at caviot-studio.vercel.app.

What we collect

Nothing. Caviot Studio has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your designs, photos, files or any other information to us or to anyone else.

In-app purchase

On iPhone and iPad, the optional Caviot Studio Pro unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy. The app checks the purchase on your device with Apple's StoreKit; we do not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Photos, camera and files

When you choose a photo, take a picture or open a 3D file, Caviot Studio reads it on your device to build your design. It stays on your device. Photo cutouts (the subject-finding model) also run on your device.

Storage on your device

The app saves your current design on your device so you can pick up where you left off. You can delete it at any time with "Start new design" or by deleting the app.

Sharing

Files leave the app only when you export or share them yourself, and they go only where you choose (for example Files, AirDrop, a folder or app you pick on Android, or your slicer).

Children

Caviot Studio is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if accounts or analytics are added), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details and the Google Play Data safety section before the change ships.
