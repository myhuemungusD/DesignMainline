Design Mainline / Caviot Studio

Support

Caviot Studio helps you design custom 3D-printable sleeves and covers. Import a sleeve template (STL, OBJ or 3MF), add logos, text or photo silhouettes, and export a print-ready STL or OBJ for your slicer.

Common questions

  • Use your own sleeve: tap "Use my own sleeve (STL)" or share a file to "Open in Caviot" from the Files app.
  • Exports: finished files are saved with Share / Save to Files.
  • Works offline: everything runs on your device.

Contact

Questions or problems: admin@designmainline.org. We usually reply within two business days.
